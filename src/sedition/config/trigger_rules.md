# Released event trigger rules

### 7.1 E01 — Retention-cell service life

> **Notify immediately when the certified service life of a component fitted in key transfer devices of an identified production lot is stated to expire at a given date and time, devices of that same lot are recorded as drawn, issued, and receipted to a unit scheduled to depart within twelve hours, and that expiry falls inside the unit's programmed rotation with no replacement action reachable before the unit returns.**

### 7.2 E02 — Off-float fuel issue

> **Notify immediately when an operating tank's floating suction assembly is recorded as off float at a fixed position near the tank floor with no deviation, waiver, or approval covering that drawing position, that same tank both received a bulk transfer and issued fuel to aircraft during or within the settling interval following that receipt, and those aircraft are scheduled to depart within twelve hours before the condition of the product placed aboard them can be established.**

### 7.3 E03 — Suspended restraint lot

> **Notify immediately when a materiel suspension notice against an identified restraint-hardware lot has been executed against the warehouse bulk store but not against the cargo-area ready-issue rack that restraint was drawn from, straps were drawn from that rack by count with no lot recorded and installed as the restraint on cargo aboard a named aircraft, and that aircraft is scheduled to depart within twelve hours with the installed lot unreadable in place.**
